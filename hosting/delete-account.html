Delete your SplitMate account

In the app

  1. Open SplitMate and go to Profile.
  2. Scroll down and tap Delete account.
  3. If you still owe money in a group, settle up first. The screen shows who you owe.
  4. Confirm with your password, or with Google if you signed in with Google, and tap Delete my account.

Without the app

Email splitmate.support123@gmail.com from the email address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my SplitMate account". We will delete your account within 30 days and reply when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, photo, friend code and UPI ID
  • Your personal expenses, categories, budgets and receipts
  • Your friends list and your notifications
  • Your sign-in (email/password or Google)

This happens immediately and can't be undone.

What is kept

Expenses and payments you shared in groups stay in those groups, because the other members' balances depend on them. You appear there as "Deleted user". Messages already delivered to other people's notification inboxes stay with them.
