SplitMate

Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026

SplitMate is an Android app for tracking personal expenses and splitting bills with friends. This policy explains what information the app stores, why, who can see it, and how you can delete it. If anything here is unclear, email splitmate.support123@gmail.com.

Information we store

  • Account: your name and email address. If you sign up with email, your password is handled by Firebase Authentication and is never visible to us. If you use Google sign-in, we receive your Google account name, email address and profile photo link.
  • Profile: your display name, an optional profile photo, your friend code and an optional UPI ID.
  • Personal expenses: amounts, descriptions, categories, dates, notes, receipt photos you attach, monthly budgets and recurring expense schedules.
  • Groups: group names and members, shared expenses and how they are split, payments you record, invitations and the group's activity history.
  • Friends: your friend connections and any nicknames you give them.
  • Notifications: in-app messages about activity that involves you.

SplitMate does not access your contacts, location, SMS or call logs, and it never asks for bank or card details or your UPI PIN. The camera and photo library are only used when you choose to add a receipt or profile photo. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking SDKs, and we do not sell your data. The only diagnostic data is the optional crash reports described below.

How we use it

Only to run the app's features: showing your spending, calculating who owes whom in a group, keeping your personal ledger in sync with your group shares, and sending you in-app notifications. We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

Who can see your information

  • Only you: your email address, your personal expenses, budgets, receipts, notifications and the nicknames you set.
  • Members of a group: everything in that group, including its expenses, splits, payments, receipts and activity history.
  • Other SplitMate users: your public profile, meaning your display name, profile photo, friend code and UPI ID if you added one. Your UPI ID is shown so that people who owe you can pay you.

Services we rely on

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Hosting) stores your account and app data on Google's servers. See Firebase's privacy information.
  • Google Sign-In, if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Firebase App Check with Google Play Integrity checks that requests to our database come from the genuine SplitMate app on a real Android device. Android sends Google a signal about the app and the device's integrity; it does not include your name, email, expenses or amounts.
  • Google Fonts supplies the app's typeface, which may be downloaded from Google's servers.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives a crash report if the app crashes: what went wrong in the code, the phone model, Android version and app version. Reports do not include your name, email, expenses or amounts. You can turn them off in Profile → Privacy & security.
  • ExchangeRate-API provides today's exchange rate when you enter an expense in another currency. Only the currency code (for example "THB") is sent.
  • Your UPI app. When you tap "Pay via UPI", SplitMate opens the UPI app you choose with the payee's UPI ID, name, the amount and a short note filled in. The payment itself happens entirely in that app. SplitMate never moves money and only records a payment after you confirm it went through.

How long we keep it

Your information is kept for as long as you have an account. Personal expenses you delete are removed. Expenses deleted in a group are hidden from the group but kept in its records so balances stay correct. Notifications you have read are removed automatically after 30 days.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the app: Profile → Delete account. If you still owe money in a group, the app asks you to settle up first, so that nobody is left unpaid.

Deleting your account immediately removes your profile, photo, friend code and UPI ID, your personal expenses, categories, budgets and receipts, your friends list, your notifications and your sign-in. Expenses you shared in groups stay in those groups so that the other members' balances remain correct; you appear there as "Deleted user". Messages already delivered to other people's notification inboxes stay with them.

If you can't use the app, email splitmate.support123@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, and we will delete your account within 30 days. More details are on the account deletion page.

Security

If you turn on App lock, SplitMate asks for your fingerprint or your phone's screen lock when it opens, and hides its preview in the recent-apps screen. This check is done by Android; your fingerprint never reaches SplitMate or our servers.

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and database access rules make sure people can only read and change what they are allowed to, such as their own data or the groups they belong to. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

SplitMate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.
